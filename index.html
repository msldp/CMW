SERVICES

Restoration work built around the problem.

From masonry and concrete repair to waterproofing and foundation work, CMW Restoration provides long-lasting solutions that restore and protect your home.

01 — Concrete

Concrete Repair & Replacement

Repair or replacement of damaged, deteriorated, or failing concrete surfaces and structures.

Foundation Repair

Address foundation concerns with a repair approach based on the condition, cause, and surrounding conditions.

02 — Masonry

Masonry Repair

Repair and restoration of damaged, deteriorated, or failing masonry.

Tuckpointing

Removal and replacement of deteriorated mortar joints to help protect and preserve masonry.

Chimney Restoration

Repair and restoration of chimney masonry, including deteriorated joints, brick and other masonry components.

03 — Waterproofing

Basement Waterproofing

Address water intrusion and moisture concerns with solutions suited to the property's conditions.

Foundation Waterproofing

Protect foundation walls from water intrusion with appropriate waterproofing methods and materials.

04 — Cleaning & Coatings

Masonry Cleaning

Clean and remove buildup, staining, and surface deterioration from masonry while taking care to protect the underlying material.

Masonry Coatings

Apply appropriate coatings to help protect masonry surfaces from moisture and the elements.
